## Enable it (you, logged in as niranjanaryan)

1. Turn on **2FA** if it is off: https://github.com/settings/security
2. Open **https://github.com/sponsors/accounts**
3. Click **Get sponsored** on your personal account
4. Country / region, then Stripe Connect (payouts)
5. Tax form
6. Profile + tiers (paste below)
7. **Request approval** / publish

When it is live, https://github.com/sponsors/niranjanaryan returns 200 and Gale shows **Sponsor**.

India is a supported Stripe region; GitHub may take a short review.
